pinnedthe grid on top. Sites you keep for good: they survive restarts, reopen in the background without loading until you click them, and stay visible (dashed) even after you close them. A tile can span several cells.

foldersa full-width row in that grid. Click it and a drawer slides open with the pinned sites inside, so "work" or "reading" take one line until you need them.

  • fastx86-64-v3 build with ThinLTO and PGO; an AVX1 build for older CPUs.
  • spacesseparate tab sets for work and life, switched in place.
  • o: notesyour markdown vault in a tab, with live preview.
  • t: terminala canvas of real shells you can pan, zoom and rearrange.
  • themesdefault and pixelized built in; import your own.
  • no accountworks fully without signing in; nothing nags you to.
