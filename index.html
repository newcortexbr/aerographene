A Chromium fork for Linux, tuned for startup and memory, with spaces, markdown notes and terminal workspaces built in. No account needed, nothing asks you to sign in. Free, GPL-3.0, made for fun.

$
  • fastx86-64-v3 build with ThinLTO and PGO; an AVX1 build for older CPUs.
  • spacesseparate tab sets for work and life, switched in place.
  • o: notesyour markdown vault in a tab, with tabs, live preview and search.
  • t: terminala canvas of real shells you can pan, zoom and rearrange.
  • themesdefault and pixelized built in; import your own.
  • no accountworks fully without signing in; nothing nags you to.

The script picks Arch, Debian, Fedora or a tarball for your system, AVX2 or AVX1 for your CPU, and checks SHA256SUMS before installing. Read it first, as you should.
